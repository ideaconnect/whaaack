# Launch post copy

Companion text for the four cards in this folder. The art comes from
`tools/generate_social_cards.py`; this is the text that goes beside it.

Every claim below is one the app keeps. Before editing, check it still holds:

| Claim | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| No tutorial — the first button starts a run | `ui/HomeScreen.kt`, "Play for fun" |
| Nothing to wait for — no energy, no timers | there is no such system in `game/` |
| One ad at most every two minutes, only between runs | `ads/AdsManager.kt`, `MIN_GAP_MS = 120_000` |
| Ad-free is one purchase, not a subscription | `billing/` |
| Three escapes end the run; score is milliseconds | `game/GameEngine.kt` |
| Speeds up every 4s; 3rd fruit at gear 5, 4th at gear 11 | `game/GameEngine.kt` |

Two housekeeping notes before posting:

* Replace `[LINK]` with `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tech.idct.whaaack`.
  On LinkedIn, put it in the first comment rather than the body if you want the reach —
  the feed demotes posts with outbound links.
* The Polish copy is written impersonally, so it carries no grammatical gender. To write
  in the first person instead, "Whaaack! robi odwrotnie" becomes "zrobiłem/zrobiłam
  odwrotnie", and so on.

## LinkedIn — English

Pair with `linkedin-en-1200x627.png`.

> There is a thing games do now that is getting hard to forgive: you open one, and before
> you are allowed to play, it takes fifteen minutes off you. A tutorial you cannot skip.
> An energy bar. "Come back in 3 hours — or watch this ad."
>
> Whaaack! does the opposite. It is free on Google Play. You open it, you tap Play, and
> you are playing. That is the entire onboarding.
>
> The game: fruit pops out of a board and you whack it before it ducks back down. Miss
> three and the run is over, and your score is simply how many milliseconds you lasted.
> It starts gentle and quietly speeds up — a third fruit joins in, then a fourth, and
> sooner or later the orchard is faster than you are. Deceptively simple, right up until
> you look up and twenty minutes have gone.
>
> The monetisation, in full: one short ad may play *between* runs — never during one, at
> most one every couple of minutes, and the game says so before it plays. If you would
> rather not, a single one-off purchase removes them. Not a subscription. No currencies,
> no lootboxes, nothing to grind, nothing to wait for.
>
> As we say in Poland: *nie samą pracą człowiek żyje* — there is more to life than work.
> Take five minutes. Whack some fruit.
>
