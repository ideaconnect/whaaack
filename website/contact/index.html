Bug reports, questions about your data, refund problems, or a screenshot of a run you are proud of — all of it lands in the same inbox.

Privacy or account requests (access, correction, deletion, a copy of your data) are handled here too. Say which app account the request is about — the email address you signed up with is enough. See the privacy policy for what we hold and for how long.

The form is delivered by Web3Forms, which processes your message on our behalf and does not use it for anything else. A plain POST is used rather than a background request so the honest thing happens if JavaScript is off: the message still sends.
