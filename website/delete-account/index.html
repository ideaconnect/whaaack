Account

Delete your account

Two ways to do it. The first takes about ten seconds and does not involve us at all.

1. In the app — instant

Open Whaaack! and go to:

Settings → Delete my account → type DELETE to confirm.

That is the whole process. It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

2. Without the app

Uninstalled already, or lost the device? Send us a request through the contact form saying that you want the account deleted. We will confirm and delete it — within one month at the outside, as the GDPR requires, and usually within a couple of days.

If your account uses an email address or Google

Give us the email address the account uses. Typing an address into a form proves nothing, so before we delete anything we reply to the address held on the account and wait for you to answer. A request about someone else's account therefore reaches them, not the person who sent it. If you have lost access to that address, say so and we will find another way to check it is you.

If you signed in with Play Games

An account created from Play Games has no email address we can write to — the address on it is a generated label at a domain that cannot receive mail — so the check above cannot work, and we will not simply take your word for it either. Instead, tell us:

  • your display name on the leaderboard, exactly as it appears; and
  • your Play Games gamer tag, if it is now different; and
  • enough about the account to be checkable — roughly when you started playing, and your best time, which only you and the board know together.

Because the only strong proof is holding the Play Games profile itself, we will ask you to do one of two things: send the request from the Google account that Play Games profile belongs to (write to us from that address, mentioning the display name), or simply reinstall the app, sign in with Play Games — which lands you back in the same account, since the player ID is what identifies it — and use Settings → Delete my account. The second takes a minute and needs nothing from us.

If neither is possible, say so in the request and we will work out another way to satisfy ourselves it is you. What we will not do is delete an account on an unverified say-so, because the only thing that would achieve is letting a stranger erase someone else's scores.

What gets deleted

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. There is no deactivated state, no grace period, and no way for us to bring an account back.

  • Your login — the authentication user and the email address on it, or, for a Play Games account, the player ID and the generated address held against it.
  • Your display name — removed, and freed for someone else to use.
  • Every score you have recorded, so you disappear from the all-time and weekly leaderboards.
  • Your local best — deleting from inside the app also clears the offline score and signs the device out, so nothing belonging to the account is left on the phone. Your sound and display settings stay, and so does the record of an ad-free purchase.

Two things outlive the deletion. Encrypted backups hold traces until the backup they sit in rotates out, and are only ever opened to restore the service after a failure. Our backend also keeps a sign-in log — the address used and the IP it came from — which is security record keeping rather than account data, and is not linked back to a profile that no longer exists.

What is not affected

  • The ad-free purchase. It belongs to your Google Play account, not to your Whaaack! account. Deleting the account does not refund it and does not take it away — it will still be there if you come back. Refunds are handled by Google Play.
  • Your Play Games profile. Achievements and run statistics sit on your Google Play Games profile, which belongs to Google, not to us — deleting a Whaaack! account leaves them exactly as they are. To clear those as well: Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, then under Delete individual game data find Whaaack! and delete it (the same thing is at play.google.com/games/profile; Google's instructions). Deleting your Whaaack! account also does not sign you out of Play Games: sign in that way again and you get a brand-new account, because the player ID was the only thing tying you to the old one and we no longer hold it.
  • Casual play. You can carry on playing for fun with no account at all.
