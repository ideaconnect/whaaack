How it plays

Simple to start. Brutal by the end.

There is one rule and one number. Everything else is the orchard steadily removing your margin for error.

Whack what pops

Two fruit cycle at first, each refilling on a jittered timer of its own, so the pair drifts in and out of step. A third joins in the sixth gear, a fourth in the seventh, a fifth in the thirteenth.

Three escapes and out

A fruit you do not reach in time is a strike. The orchard pulses red at its edges every single time one gets away — never over the middle, where you are still looking. Three of them end the run.

Milliseconds, not points

Your score is exactly how long you lasted — no multipliers, no combo maths. A casual run needs no account and never leaves the device; sign in and a ranked one posts its time to a global and a weekly board.

It never levels off

Fruit arrive faster and live shorter every four seconds, another joins every four gears, and neither curve ever eases: by top speed all sixteen tiles cycle. Take a call and the clock stops — though a closed app ends the run.

Ad-free

Remove ads

A single purchase, not a subscription. It is tied to your Google Play account, so it comes back on a reinstall or a new phone — there is a Restore purchases button in Settings for exactly that. Full detail in the terms.

Without it, a short full-screen ad may play between runs — at most one every couple of minutes, and never during one. The game tells you before it does, and offers the unlock at the same moment.
