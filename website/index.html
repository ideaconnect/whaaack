How it plays

Simple to start. Brutal by the end.

There is one rule and one number. Everything else is the orchard steadily removing your margin for error.

Whack what pops

Two fruit cycle at first, on their own timers so they never blink in unison. A third joins in the sixth gear, a fourth in the seventh.

Three escapes and out

A fruit you do not reach in time is a strike. Three strikes end the run — the whole orchard flashes red so you never wonder what just happened.

Milliseconds, not points

Your score is exactly how long you lasted. No multipliers, no combo maths — nothing between you and the number.

It keeps tightening

Fruit arrive faster and live shorter every four seconds, until the curve bottoms out at top speed — the point where it genuinely stops getting harder.

Ranked or just for fun

Casual runs need no account at all and work offline. Sign in and your best run lands on a global and a weekly leaderboard.

Fair when you come back

Take a call mid-run and the clock stops dead. You return to a countdown, not to a board that has been running without you.

Ad-free

One tap, one price, forever.

Remove ads

A single purchase, not a subscription. It is tied to your Google Play account, so it follows you to a new phone — and if you are ever offline, you keep it. We would rather give an ad-free run to someone who has not paid than take one away from someone who has.

🎈

Free is a real game

Nothing is locked behind the purchase. No energy, no timers, no paid revives — the free game is the whole game.

⏱️

Ads stay out of the way

One interstitial between runs at most, capped to roughly one every couple of minutes. Never mid-run, never on launch.

🔒

Your call on tracking

Consent is asked before any ad loads, and you can change your mind at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options.

Your account

Optional, and genuinely deletable.

Play without one

Casual runs never touch the network. Your best score lives on the device and nowhere else.

Sign in only to rank

An account exists for one reason: putting your name on the leaderboard. We store your email, that display name, and your scores. Nothing else.

Delete it in two taps

Settings → Delete my account erases the account, the email, every score, and the local best too. It is immediate and irreversible — no “freeze”, no waiting period. Delete without the app →
