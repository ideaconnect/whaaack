2. What the service is

Whaaack! is a single-player arcade game. It is free to download and free to play in full. Three optional extras exist:

  • an account, which is required only to place scores on the public leaderboard;
  • Google Play Games, which adds achievements and profile statistics, and which can also stand in for an account — see section 3; and

None is required to play, and nothing about the gameplay is locked behind any of them. Play Games in particular is not ours: your device signs in to it by itself if it has a profile, achievements are unlocked from casual play as readily as ranked, and signing out of Play Games switches all of that off without affecting the game. What it sends to Google is set out in the privacy policy.

3. Your account

There are three ways to have one, and they differ in what you give us:

  • Email and password — you must give an address you control, confirm it, and keep the password to yourself.
  • Google — Google confirms who you are and we receive your address and name. There is no separate password.
  • Play Games — your Google Play Games profile identifies the account. There is no email address and no password: signing in with Play Games again is the only way in, on any device. Your Play Games gamer tag becomes your starting display name on the public leaderboard, and you can change it afterwards like anyone else.

Whichever you use:

  • One person, one account. Do not create accounts to inflate or manipulate the board. Note that an email or Google account and a Play Games account are separate accounts even when they are the same person — we have no way to know they are you both times, and we do not merge them. Keeping two of your own is not cheating; using several to occupy the board is, and falls under section 4.
  • Your display name is public. It must be 2 to 24 characters long, made only of unaccented letters a–z, digits, spaces, dots, underscores and hyphens, and it must start and end with a letter or a digit. It must also be unique, ignoring capitalisation. Where the name comes from your Play Games gamer tag, we adjust it automatically to fit those rules — trimming what they do not allow, and adding a number if the name is already taken — so the name on the board may differ from your gamer tag. Beyond that it must not be offensive, impersonate anyone, contain contact details or advertising, or infringe anyone's rights. We may replace a name that breaks the last rule with a neutral one — every account has to have a name, so there is nothing to delete — and we will tell you why. To report someone else's name, use the contact form.

  • If you are under 16, do not create an account without a parent or guardian's agreement. Casual play requires no account.
  • You can delete your account at any time — see section 10.

We can also delete a score after the event, and in serious or repeated cases suspend the account. Where we do that we will say so if you ask.

What you get

Advertising is removed from the app for as long as Google Play reports the purchase as yours: there is no expiry and nothing to renew, and the only thing that ends it is the purchase itself being refunded and revoked. The entitlement is tied to the Google Play account that bought it, so it follows you to a new device and survives a reinstall. Use Settings → Restore purchases if it does not appear immediately.

We deliberately err on your side. The one exception is a fresh install that has never managed to ask: it has nothing to go on, so it shows ads until it reaches Play once.
